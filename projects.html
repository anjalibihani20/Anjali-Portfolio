Projects

Three concepts I've designed as a way to practice turning a specific problem into a working plan and prototype. Each one started from something I noticed rather than a generic tutorial idea. None of these have live screenshots yet, so the previews below are concept sketches, not finished product screens.

Concept preview

Ghar ka Swaad

Web concept · HTML, CSS, JavaScript

People living away from home often know exactly how they want their food to taste, but explaining that preference to someone else isn't always easy.

Ghar ka Swaad explores a voice-first way to turn those everyday food preferences into clearer instructions for the cook.

Speak→Understand→Customize→Cook→Remember

"Mirchi bahut kam, salt medium, oil low, dal thodi thin."

Interpreted as — Spice: Low, Salt: Medium, Oil: Low, Consistency: Thin.

Concept preview

Campus Lost & Found

Web concept · HTML, CSS, JavaScript

A simple campus platform for reporting, browsing and finding lost items without making the process complicated.

The goal is to replace scattered WhatsApp messages and physical noticeboards with a single, searchable place where students can post a lost or found item and match with each other faster. It's a small problem, but a real one — most students have lost something on campus at least once.

Concept preview

Walk This Way

Web concept · HTML, CSS, JavaScript

A navigation concept focused on helping people feel more confident while travelling by combining route information with safety alerts and quick access to emergency contacts.

The idea came from thinking through what actually helps someone feel safer in the moment — not just a map, but alerts, context, and a quick way to reach an emergency contact.
